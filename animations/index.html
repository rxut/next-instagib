How IG+ draws player models

UT blends a model from one move to the next by sliding every point in a straight line. On some moves that folds the legs or pulls an arm through the body for a moment. IG+ fixes the worst of these.

Every clip plays at a quarter speed. The base game is on the left and IG+ is on the right. Both are filmed at the same moment and spot.

Left: base gameRight: IG+ with the fixes

A model freezes for a moment, then jumps

Sometimes the game holds a player's model still for a split second and then jumps it to the next pose.

IG+ blends it like any other move.

Legs fold when a player switches strafe direction

The game moves every point of the model in a straight line from the old pose to the new one. Switching strafe direction makes the legs cross and go thin for a moment.

IG+ has new in-between frames for this move, made from each model's own frames. The legs and the chest turn and keep their shape. The head and the gun move exactly as before.

The same, the other way

Legs fold when a player stops strafing

Stopping a strafe folds the legs the same way. The arm that swings back to the gun also passes through the chest.

IG+ turns the legs and the arm instead. The gun stays where the game puts it.

The arm disappears into the chest when a player starts strafing

Starting a strafe from standing pulls that arm through the body, and the legs go thin.

IG+ keeps the arm in front of the chest before it swings behind the body. The legs keep their shape.

Legs fold when a player lands into a strafe

Landing and strafing away right after folds the legs and the arm.

IG+ turns them instead.

Legs fold going from a crouch into a strafe

The game uses the crouch pose in the air as well, so this shows up after jumps and dodges too.

IG+ turns the legs instead.

A forward flip lands as a pancake

A forward flip often lands before its animation ends. The game then blends the model straight from upside down to standing. For a moment it is flat.

IG+ plays the flip to its end first, then lands at the base game's pace. The free hand keeps its size on its way back to the gun.

Only how the model is drawn changes. The moves and their timing are the same.

The new frames are made for the five stock models and for IG+'s forced skins. Custom models keep the old blend.
