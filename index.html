Next Netcode InstaGib vs NewNet InstaGib

NewNet
Your game client decides the hit. The server trusts it with almost no checks.
Next InstaGib
The server tests every shot itself at the spot your screen showed.

Where both work the same

With clean connections and normal ping a single shot works the same in both.

  • Your beam and ring show the moment you click and they use your own beam settings.
  • The shot counts at the spot where your screen showed the other player.
  • You can still die a step behind a wall.
  • Trades are a server setting in both. With trades off the first frag stands and a dead player's shot does not count.

Where they differ

They differ in six cases. Each one has its own section below.

  • 01
    A player's connection is uneven.
  • 02
    An update arrives late during a dodge.
  • 03
    A door closes behind you, or the shooter's delay is above the server's limit.
  • 04
    A game client is modified.
  • 05
    The packet with a shot is lost on a high ping.
  • 06
    Two players click at almost the same moment.

What you have seen with NewNet and what changes

01

Players with a bad connection jitter, skip and teleport

NewNet
Packets on time. The player moves.
Packets late. The player stands still.
Packets arrive at once. A jump.
On their own screen
On your screen

The server runs each move the moment its packet arrives. When a player's packets come late and all at once, that player stands still and then covers the missed time in one tick.

Next InstaGib
Moves play evenly, a little later.
On their own screen
On your screen

Adaptive Buffering queues that player's moves and plays them at the pace the player made them. Only that player gets extra delay, up to 100 ms. Their scoreboard ping includes it. A move later than that still shows as a jump. A clean connection is not queued.

Why Next is better

That player runs evenly on your screen and you can aim at them.

Your game moves every other player forward at their last speed until the next update arrives. That is a guess. When an update is late during a dodge, the guess carries the dodge on. Then your game pulls the player back.

Next InstaGib
The player dodges.
Update late. A short stop.
Update arrives. No pull back.
Where the player really is
Where your game draws them
End of the dodge

Tick Interpolation draws every other player between two real server updates, a little in the past. When an update is late your game carries the player on for two server ticks at most. Then they stand still. Body, animation and crouch come from the same moment. Your shot tells the server which moment you saw, and the server tests the players there.

Why Next is better

You see where a player really ran. A late update now shows as a short stop.

03

You are behind a wall or a door and you still die

NewNet
How old the spot can be
now
no limit

The shooter's game tests the shot at the spot where it drew you. You die for the spot you stood on about two pings ago, the shooter's ping plus yours. NewNet has no limit on it. The shooter's game also decides about doors, so a shot can pass a door that has already closed on the server.

Next InstaGib
How old the spot can be
now
250 ms by default

Precise Rewind Time puts every player back where the shooter's screen showed them. It goes back up to a limit the server sets, 250 ms by default. So you can still die a step behind a wall. Doors are not put back. A door blocks the shot once it is closed on the server, even if it still looks open to the shooter.

Why Next is better

A shot can't reach a spot older than the server allows, whatever the shooter's ping. A closed door stops it.

04

You die through a wall and can't tell lag from a cheat

NewNet

No

No

No

The server checks one thing. By default the reported hit point must be within 10000 units of a spot you stood on in the last half second. It checks no wall and no fire rate. A modified game could report a hit through a wall and fire faster than the rifle allows. Only an anti-cheat tool can catch that.

Next InstaGib
Shot traced by the server
Yes
Wall between shooter and target
Yes
Time between two shots
Yes

The server traces every shot itself from the shooter's eye along the aim. It takes no game's word for a hit and it keeps the time between two shots itself.

Why Next is better

Every frag has passed the server's own trace. A modified game can't report hits of its own or fire faster. An aimbot can still aim, so this does not stop every cheat.

05

Your beam goes through a player and nothing happens

NewNet
Shot packet lost
Sent again
Over 0.1 s late
Shot dropped

Your game sends each shot in one packet and sends it again if that packet is lost. The server drops a shot that is more than 0.1 seconds behind your newest movement. On a high ping the second copy can come too late. With Hit Source set to Client you still heard the hit sound.

Next InstaGib
Shot packet lost
Next packet arrives
Server fires the shot

The shot travels with your movement. If the packet with the shot is lost the server fires the shot on your next packet.

Why Next is better

A lost packet only delays your shot. With NewNet it can cost you the shot.

06

You both click at almost the same moment

NewNet
One server tick. A clicked first. B's packet arrived first.
B's shot runs first
B gets the frag

The server runs shots in the order their packets arrive. So the later click can win because its packet arrived first.

Next InstaGib
One server tick. A clicked first. B's packet arrived first.
A's shot runs first
A gets the frag

Fire Time Order runs the shots of one server tick in the order the players fired them. It adds no delay.

Why Next is better

Inside one tick the player who clicked first wins. With NewNet the packet that arrived first wins. It is a small gain. A tick is 10 ms at 100 ticks a second. Across ticks the shot that arrives first still runs first. That is usually the shot of the player with the lower ping.

What you will notice

With Hit Source set to Client the hit sound still plays at the click. It comes from your own game's test and the server's decision is final. A sound can play for a shot the server counts as a miss. A hit that only the server saw plays its sound a ping late.
